# House style: the Index's voice

Every line a player reads while playing should sound like it came from the same place. The story states the theme: **a deadpan
bureaucracy of the impossible.** The Cosmic Index of Summonable Persons files everything, accepts most things, never takes sides and
wants a receipt. This page is for whoever writes the next line (usually Claude, sometimes Aaron).

## The voice

- **Deadpan.** Short sentences. No exclamation marks. Understatement, never a shout: "The Index is not hopeful."
- **Third person.** The Index speaks of itself ("The Index has noted..."), of Zennit by name, and of "the group" or the player by name.
  Never "you must".
- **It never takes sides.** When it helps someone it says so with a straight face: "The Index, which takes no sides, has cut his edge."
- **Paperwork is its vocabulary.** Filed, stamped, noted, countersigned, entered, evidence, receipt, triplicate, the tab, the ledger,
  the clerk, the Ritual, Form 27B slash 6.
- **Facts first.** A line carries its numbers and names plainly; the voice goes around them, not instead of them. Every variant of
  a line must say the same facts.

## Words

| Say | Rather than | Because |
|---|---|---|
| a summons, two summons | a summon, two summons' | The Index is formal; "summons" is both |
| filed under enthusiasm | ignored, over the limit | The cap has a name |
| filed as filler | skipped, void | His week off has a name |
| the tab | the debt, what you owe | The silver is a tab, kept without a receipt |
| stamped (a card, a punch) | used, redeemed | The Index stamps things |
| noted, entered, framed | recorded, saved, logged | |
| leave | holiday, vacation | Zennit goes on leave |
| the week closes | the week ends | The Index closes things |

## Plain on purpose

Not everything needs the voice. These stay plain, because they are for finding things or fixing them:
errors and `/sc errors`, `/sc help`, tips, the admin and test tools, button and tab labels, the playtest report, and any line a
player must read at a glance (the "Week:" line leads with its numbers; one of its three variants is plain).

## Five examples

| Plain | The Index |
|---|---|
| Bo paid you 200s by trade. Mark 1 summons paid? | Bo has paid you 200s by trade. The Index would like to mark 1 of Bo's summons paid. |
| 2 summons are waiting for your answer. | The Index is holding 2 summons for your answer. |
| Bo owes 250 silver on 2 summons. | The Index has Bo down for 250 silver on 2 summons. |
| The week closes in 3 hours. | The Index closes the week in 3 hours. |
| A card pays the silver; 4 punches left. | Bo's card pays the 40 silver: the Index stamps it, 4 punches left. |

## Checking a new line

1. Could the Index say it? If it needs an exclamation mark, it is not the Index.
2. Does a variant that is plain still carry every fact? (The self-tests read the first variant, which is the plain one.)
3. Is it a line people read *while playing*? Then voice it. Is it a label or a tool? Leave it plain.
